This ER diagram is related to a course Registration..
there are five entities.
Entity - Student, Enrollment, Lecture, Lecturer, and Subjects

the subject entity attributes are F_name, L_name, B_date, No
The Enrillment entity related attributes are Subject_num, Date_of_enrolment, Course_name, CC#
The Lecture entity related attributes are CC#, Subject, Time, Date, Lecture_name
The Subject entity related attributes are Subject_code, Subject_unit, Subject_udsc
The Lecture entity related arrtributes are Lecture_id, L_lastname, L_firstname, L_email, L_address

There are six relationships.
one to one relation between Student and Entroll Entities.
one to many relation between Lecture and Student.
one to many relation between Lecture and Entrollment.
many to many relation between Lecturer and Subjects.
many to many relation between Lecture and Lecturer.
many to many relation between Subjects and Lecturer.
